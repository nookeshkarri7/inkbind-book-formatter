Inkbind, the offline book formatter

Inkbind turns a Word manuscript into a KDP-ready print PDF and an EPUB on your own Windows PC or Android phone. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and the Windows Pro upgrade is a one-time $79.

Why authors use it

  • Windows, natively. No rented Mac and no browser tab that needs the internet to import or export.
  • Pages that match the print file. Chapters open on right-hand pages, with drop caps, running heads and no widows or orphans.
  • KDP's paperback rules built in. Trim sizes from 5 × 8 to 7 × 10 inches, bleed, and an inside margin set from the page count.
  • An ebook from the same file. EPUB 3 with a clickable table of contents and embedded fonts.
  • Series-ready. Templates keep every book in a series looking the same; use your own fonts on Windows Pro.
